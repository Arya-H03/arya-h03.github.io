About Me


Hi! I'm Arya, a Junior Game Programmer based in Montreal, Canada. With a strong foundation in Unity and Unreal Engine I have worked on a variety of game projects and most recently started making my own custom engine to get a better insight into lower level game programming.

My skillset is mainly focused around C++ and C#, with Lua as a scripting language. However I also have some prior knowledge with web development and database management.

My aim is to create fun games by combining my passion and talent for programming with my love for video games, all while focusing on developing quality software. Beside programming, I enjoy weightlifting, combat sports and gaming!
